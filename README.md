## 👨‍💻 About me

I'm a software engineer, founder, and educator who enjoys turning complex technical problems into useful products—and making the lessons accessible to other developers.

- 🚀 **Founder of [Teachyst](https://www.teachyst.com/)** — helping educators and creators host, brand, and monetize their courses on their own domains.
- 🎙️ **Building [Vidyst](https://vidyst.com/)** — a live streaming, webinar, and recording platform designed to make hosting online events feel effortless.
- 🎥 **Teaching 426K+ developers** through [YouTube](https://youtube.com/@piyushgargdev), with practical tutorials on backend engineering, full-stack development, DevOps, system design, and AI.
- 🤖 Exploring **AI agents, agentic workflows, and developer tools**—with a focus on products that solve real problems.
- ⚙️ Interested in **distributed systems, real-time communication, cloud infrastructure, and scalable backends**.
- ✍️ I learn by building, share what I learn, and believe great engineering should be understandable.

> **My philosophy:** Build things that matter. Understand how they work. Share what you learn.
